AI (Artificial Intelligence)

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science concerned with building machines and systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. This includes learning, reasoning, perception, problem-solving, and even creativity.

Modern AI primarily focuses on Machine Learning and Deep Learning, where models can learn from data and make predictions and decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

The ultimate goal of AI is to create systems that can think, learn, and act autonomously.

Common Uses of Artificial Intelligence:

  • Voice Assistants & Chatbots: Like Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant, and ChatGPT, which use Natural Language Processing (NLP).
  • Recommendation Systems: The algorithms behind Netflix, Amazon, and YouTube that suggest personalized content.
  • Computer Vision: Facial recognition in photos, object detection in self-driving cars, and medical image analysis.
  • Healthcare & Medicine: Assisting in disease diagnosis, drug discovery, and analyzing medical images like MRIs and CT Scans.
  • Cybersecurity: Identifying patterns of cyber attacks and anomalies in networks.
  • Process Automation: Automating repetitive and complex administrative tasks (RPA).
  • Marketing & Sales: Predicting customer behavior, optimizing ad campaigns, and dynamic pricing.
