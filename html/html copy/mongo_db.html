MongoDB

MongoDB is a popular NoSQL, document-oriented database. Unlike relational (SQL) databases that use tables and rows, MongoDB stores data in JSON-like documents with dynamic structure.

This feature makes it very flexible and scalable for storing unstructured or semi-structured data.

Common Uses of MongoDB:

  • Real-time Applications: Such as chat applications, real-time analytics, and IoT.
  • Content Management & Blogging Platforms: Due to flexibility in storing various content types.
  • Scalable Mobile & Web Apps: That require horizontal scaling.
  • Storing Catalog & Product Data: Which may not have a fixed structure and can change.
